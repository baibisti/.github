## Publication rule

Public visibility documents the work; it does not approve any translation for campaign use. Treat `high`, `medium`, and `low` as editorial confidence—not native certification. Before using any localized motto in product, campaign, packaging, or clothing, record native-copy review, locale, script, market, and approval date.
